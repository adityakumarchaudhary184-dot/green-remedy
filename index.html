Green Remedy

A Simple Guide to 15 Everyday Herbs, Plants, and Their Health Benefits

Welcome to Green Remedy

This website is designed to help anyone learn about the amazing health benefits of common plants. Nature provides wonderful, traditional home remedies that anyone can prepare easily to support their daily wellness!

1. Tulsi (Holy Basil)

Scientific Name: Ocimum tenuiflorum

Common Uses: Colds, coughs, and respiratory infections; immunity booster.

  • Helps cure common cough, cold, and sore throat.
  • Boiled in water to make a traditional herbal tea.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Boil 5-6 Tulsi leaves with a small piece of crushed ginger. Strain it, add a spoonful of honey, and drink warm to soothe a bad cough.

2. Aloe Vera

Scientific Name: Aloe barbadensis miller

Common Uses: Soothing minor burns, skin cuts, and moisturizing skin; mild digestion support.

  • Excellent for moisturizing dry skin and improving hair health.
  • Often consumed fresh as a juice to aid digestion in the morning.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Cut open a fresh Aloe Vera leaf and apply the clear, inner gel directly onto minor skin burns or sunburns for instant cooling and healing.

3. Ashwagandha (Indian Ginseng)

Scientific Name: Withania somnifera

Common Uses: Reducing stress/anxiety, improving energy and stamina, and sleep support.

  • Helps the body manage stress and calms the nervous system.
  • Promotes deep, natural sleep and restores energy levels.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Mix half a teaspoon of Ashwagandha root powder into a glass of warm milk with a little honey, and drink it before bed for a relaxing sleep.

4. Neem

Scientific Name: Azadirachta indica

Common Uses: Treating skin acne, fungal/bacterial rashes, and blood purification.

  • Leaves treat skin infections, pimples, and rashes due to antibacterial properties.
  • Acts as a completely natural insect and mosquito repellent.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Boil a handful of Neem leaves in your bathing water to soothe skin allergies, rashes, and body acne naturally.

5. Turmeric (Haldi)

Scientific Name: Curcuma longa

Common Uses: Anti-inflammatory, healing cuts/wounds, and boosting immunity.

  • Contains strong natural components that fight internal and external inflammation.
  • Speeds up the recovery process for minor cuts, internal pain, and bruises.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Mix half a teaspoon of turmeric powder into warm milk (Golden Milk) and drink it at night to relieve body aches and boost immunity. For cuts, apply a thick paste of turmeric and water directly to the skin.

6. Ginger (Adrak)

Scientific Name: Zingiber officinale

Common Uses: Relieving nausea, digestive cramps, and cold/throat congestion.

  • Quickly settles an upset stomach and reduces motion sickness or morning nausea.
  • Clears mucus congestion in the throat during cold weather.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Chew a thin slice of fresh ginger with a pinch of salt to stop nausea, or boil crushed ginger in water to make a warming tea for throat congestion.

7. Brahmi

Scientific Name: Bacopa monnieri

Common Uses: Enhancing memory, focus, and reducing anxiety/nervous tension.

  • Traditionally used to improve alertness, learning capability, and memory retention.
  • Calms mental fatigue and reduces emotional stress.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Consume a few fresh Brahmi leaves daily in the morning, or mix a small amount of Brahmi powder into warm water or milk to keep your mind sharp and calm.

8. Amla (Indian Gooseberry)

Scientific Name: Phyllanthus emblica

Common Uses: High source of Vitamin C, immunity, and hair/skin health.

  • Packed with vital Vitamin C to shield your body against seasonal infections.
  • Nourishes the hair roots, controls graying, and gives a healthy glow to skin.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Drink a spoonful of fresh Amla juice mixed with water every morning on an empty stomach to improve digestion and strengthen your daily immunity.

9. Giloy

Scientific Name: Tinospora cordifolia

Common Uses: Managing chronic and viral/dengue fevers, boosting immunity.

  • Well-known for helping the body manage fevers and increasing blood platelet counts.
  • Acts as a strong antioxidant to clear out bodily toxins.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Boil a small piece of Giloy stem (or a teaspoon of Giloy powder) in a glass of water until the liquid reduces to half. Strain and drink once a day during viral seasons.

10. Ajwain (Carom Seed Plant)

Scientific Name: Trachyspermum ammi

Common Uses: Relieving indigestion, gas/bloating, and nasal congestion.

  • Provides fast relief from heavy stomach bloating, gas, and digestive cramps.
  • Inhaling its steam helps open blocked nasal passages.
💡 Easy Home Remedy: Chew half a teaspoon of Ajwain seeds with a pinch of black salt and swallow with warm water for instant relief from stomach ache or gas.
